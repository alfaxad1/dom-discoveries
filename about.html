About Us

Your Gateway to Unforgettable Travel Experiences

Years of Expertise Proudly Serving Travelers for 5+

Dom Global Discoveries Limited is a registered Kenyan Tours, Travel, and Travel Management Company based in Nairobi CBD, offering reliable and comprehensive travel solutions to individuals, groups, and corporate clients. We specialize in local and international tours and safaris, airline ticketing, hotel reservations, visa application assistance, airport transfers, and corporate travel management. Our services are designed to make travel seamless, affordable, and stress-free, whether for leisure, business, or group travel. With a strong understanding of the regional and international travel landscape, Dom Global Discoveries Limited is committed to delivering personalized travel experiences backed by professional service, trusted partnerships, and customer-focused support. We pride ourselves on integrity, efficiency, and attention to detail, ensuring every journey is carefully planned and expertly managed from start to finish.

  • Reliable Airline, Hotel, and Tour Partnerships
  • Transparent Pricing with No Hidden Costs
  • Professional Support from Booking to Return
